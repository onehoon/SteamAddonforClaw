Rumble v1 planning status

The former four-PR rumble sequence is historical planning context and is
superseded by the current three-PR plan wherever they differ.

PR1 establishes the manufacturer-independent TwoMotorRumble contract, the
feedback authority, the Steam Deck 0xEB source decoder, and explicit callback
registration/clear capability. It does not register the callback in the live
production route and does not write physical HID output.

Generic rumble remains two independent 16-bit channels: LargeMotor and
SmallMotor. Source decoding and physical sinks remain separate, with one
feedback authority controlling accepted generations. Physical MSI rumble is a
later PR2/PR3 concern; no hardware validation is claimed here.

Steam Haptic 0xEA, Haptic Pulse 0x8F, audio/jingle commands, and unknown output
commands remain unsupported in Rumble v1. Gyro/IMU remain separate. The
VIIPER native ABI, generated header, DLL, and pinned dependency are unchanged.
